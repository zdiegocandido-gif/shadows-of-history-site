Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy governs the private creator utility DarkYT Command Center operated for the Shadows of History Archive channel.

1. Information Accessed via Google / YouTube APIs

When authorized by the channel owner via Google OAuth 2.0, the application accesses YouTube Data API v3 and YouTube Analytics API strictly to:

  • Upload and schedule videos created by the channel owner to the owner's authorized YouTube channel.
  • Retrieve channel statistics, watch time, retention metrics, and estimated revenue solely for display inside the owner's private dashboard.

2. Data Storage & No Third-Party Sharing

OAuth credentials and channel analytics snapshots are stored privately by the channel owner (locally and in private encrypted repository secrets). No Google user data is sold, shared, transferred, or disclosed to any third parties.

3. Google API Services User Data Policy (Limited Use)

DarkYT Command Center's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs strictly adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

4. Revoking Access & Contact

You can revoke OAuth permissions at any time from your Google Account Permissions page. For privacy questions or data deletion requests, contact zdiegocandido@gmail.com.
